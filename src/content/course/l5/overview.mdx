## What you're building

Customers of a clinic ask questions: *"Can I move my appointment to Thursday?"*, *"What happens if I cancel late?"*, *"Am I booked for the 14th?"* Support Copilot answers them from the clinic's own policies and the customer's own bookings — and knows when to stop and fetch a human.

It is a feature **inside a product that is already live**, which is the other half of the lesson.

## Why this project, at this point

Two things arrive together, deliberately:

- **Part 5's fleet reality.** Book-a-Slot is in maintenance and still has users. You are adding a significant feature to a running product while not letting it rot — the exact situation lesson 5.2 described.
- **Architecture A5.** This is the first time your software's output reaches a customer without you reading it first.

| A5 lesson | Where it lands here |
|---|---|
| Shapes of AI features | Almost all of this is retrieval, not an agent loop |
| Retrieval | Clinic policies, measured with recall@5 before anything else |
| Evals and guardrails | A CI gate on prompt changes; refusals enforced in code |
| Cost and latency | A per-request budget that survives real traffic |

## The shape of the product

Deliberately small, deliberately bounded:

- **Answers questions** from clinic policy documents and from the customer's own bookings.
- **Looks up** a booking — read-only, authorised as the customer.
- **Hands over** to a human, with the conversation attached.
- **Never** cancels, reschedules, refunds, or gives medical advice. Those are on the do-not-decide list, enforced in code.

## Done when

- Recall@5 on a 30-question set is measured, improved and recorded.
- An eval set of 40 cases runs in CI on every prompt, model or retrieval change.
- The copilot refuses what it must refuse, proven by cases in the eval set.
- A prompt injection in a support ticket does not change its behaviour.
- Cost per conversation is inside budget at real traffic, with a per-user daily ceiling in code.
- It is behind a flag, was rolled out gradually, and Book-a-Slot's existing alerts stayed quiet.
