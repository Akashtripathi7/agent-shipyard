## Recap

- **Classify before you fix:** isolated bug, polluted session, or wrong foundation. Each needs a different response.
- **Reproduce, isolate, hypothesise, prove, regression test, fix the cause.** Never hide a symptom.
- **Recover sessions deliberately:** stop, write a handoff with "tried and failed", get back to safety, move decisions into files, restart.
- **Commit whenever something works;** checkpoints, commits and branches are your safety nets.
- **Understand before changing inherited code:** audit, enrol, protect with characterization tests, then a small first change.
